rate · skew

mp_03
rate
repeat size inside the zone · smooth, with doppler when repeats are up

lit: rate pushes L and R apart · press again: locks the spread
clear skew

not skewed = ping pong (inputs summed) · skewed = swap L/R feedback · window flashes
μ
doppler only · same small range in every zone · 1 V/oct in zone 0
